What the research found

  • ETF momentum rotation held up best. 2007-2026: +11.9%/yr vs SPY's +11.0%, worst drop -17% vs -55%. Similar results with top 2/3/4 picks, with or without QQQ, and in both decades.
  • Risk parity + trend is the smoothest. Only +5.3%/yr, but the worst drop was -7%. Think of it as an alternative to holding bonds.
  • BTC trend rules cut crashes, not returns. 2019-2026: about the same return as holding, worst drop -46% vs -77%. It lagged plain holding in 2023-26.
  • Rotating between altcoins lost. Momentum across 12 coins: ~15%/yr with -86% drawdowns. Fees and alt-season collapses eat it.
  • ML models were coin flips. Logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting, retrained yearly: AUC ~0.50 on SPY and BTC. On BTC every model lost money; a 200-day average beat them all.
  • Polymarket longshot bias didn't last. Buying NO on 2-10c outcomes earned +2.5%/bet in 2023-24 but lost 1-4% on recent markets.
